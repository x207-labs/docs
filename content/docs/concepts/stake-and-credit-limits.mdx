---
title: Stake and credit limits
description: A member's credit limit is bounded by its stake. The formula, defaults, and what x207 does not promise.
---

Every member locks a **stake** in USDT. The stake is the cash that covers the member's debts. A member's **credit limit** says how much it can owe at once.

## The rule

```text
limit <= stake
```

The limit never exceeds the stake. A debtor's own stake therefore covers the debt if it defaults.

## The formula

```text
limit = min(limit_cap,
            stake * base_limit_ratio / 10000 + settled_history * limit_growth / 10000,
            stake)
```

Defaults: `base_limit_ratio` 8000, `limit_growth` 1000, `limit_cap` 1,000,000,000 (1000 credit). `settled_history` grows only when a member settles in cash. Cycle clearing does not raise it, so trading in circles to inflate a limit does not work.

So a new member starts at 80% of its stake, and the limit grows with a record of cash settlement, up to the stake and the cap. A deployed circle can use other parameters.

## Headroom

```text
headroom = max(0, limit - outstanding_debt - bundle reservations)
```

Headroom is what a member can still spend now.

## What this costs

Capital locked is at least 125% of the debt a member can run up (stake over limit at the default ratio). x207 is not undercollateralised credit. It is netting with collateral: less value moves, the stake is the guarantee, and credit above stake is not built in the current version.

## Leaving

A member calls `request_exit`, waits out the circle's exit rules, then `withdraw_stake`.
